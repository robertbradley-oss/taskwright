THE HABIT

Treat an answer as a draft.

AI can combine accurate details with one unsupported instruction. A fluent answer is a starting point, not evidence that every step is correct.

1. Isolate the claim

Look closely at numbers, conditions, and instructions. What is the answer asking the customer to do?

2. Find the exact evidence

Check the source for this model and situation. A nearby instruction may apply to a different task.

3. Correct, then set a boundary

Write what the source supports. Say when to contact support instead of inventing another step.

CUSTOMER TICKET · FICTIONAL

“The light is still amber.”

Hi, I replaced the cartridge in my Morrow Desk Filter D2. The indicator is still amber. I haven’t pressed the indicator button since replacing the cartridge. Do I need another cartridge, or is there a step I missed?

— Alex

YOUR SOURCE

Morrow D2 · Manual excerpt

Fictional training material · Revision 1

§1 · After replacing a cartridge
With the unit powered on and the new cartridge installed, hold the indicator button for 8 seconds to reset the cartridge timer. The indicator turns green when the reset succeeds.
§2 · Quick indicator check
Hold the indicator button for 3 seconds to check the current cartridge status. This check does not reset the timer.
§3 · If the light stays amber
If the indicator remains amber after one 8-second reset attempt, contact support. Do not recommend another cartridge replacement based on the light alone.
PREPARED AI DRAFT · VERIFY THIS
“Thanks for replacing the cartridge. With the unit powered on and the new cartridge installed, hold the indicator button for 3 seconds to reset the timer. The light should turn green. If it stays amber after that reset attempt, contact support. You do not need to buy another cartridge based on the light alone.”
1. Which claim needs correcting?
2. Which section gives the correct reset procedure?
3. What if one correct reset attempt fails?

Use your own words. Include the supported instruction and what to do if it fails. Your writing will be reviewed with a checklist, not automatically graded.

ONE SUPPORTED RESPONSE

Clear steps. A sensible stopping point.

Hi Alex, the amber light alone doesn’t mean you need another cartridge. With the unit powered on and your new cartridge installed, hold the indicator button for 8 seconds to reset the timer. It turns green if the reset succeeds. A 3-second hold only checks the status. If the light stays amber after one 8-second attempt, please contact support so we can help with the next step.

Evidence: §1 gives the reset conditions and duration; §2 explains the mistake; §3 sets the escalation boundary.
