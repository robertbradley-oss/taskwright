# Practice 02: Check the source

Scenario revision 1. All products and people are fictional.

## Short lesson

An instruction can be accurately quoted and still be wrong for the customer. Identify the customer's exact model, find the section that applies to it, and check the conditions before recommending an action. Similar model names do not make instructions interchangeable. When the source does not support another action, explain the next supported step.

## Ticket

“Where is the wireless button?” — Sam

I have a Vale Label Printer L4 USB. That exact model name is on the underside label. I want to print from my laptop over Wi-Fi. I haven't bought an adapter or tried any setup steps yet. How do I connect it?

## Manual excerpt — revision 1

§1 · L4 USB connection

The L4 USB prints from a computer through a USB cable. It has no Wi-Fi radio or wireless setup button. A USB Wi-Fi adapter does not add wireless printing to this model. For printing from a laptop, connect the included USB cable and follow the L4 USB driver guide for that laptop's operating system.

§2 · L4 Air wireless connection

The L4 Air supports Wi-Fi. Hold its wireless button until the blue light flashes, then follow the L4 Air setup guide. These wireless instructions apply only to the L4 Air.

§3 · When wireless printing is required

If an L4 USB owner needs wireless printing, contact support to discuss compatible models. This excerpt does not establish return eligibility, exchange availability, stock, or pricing. Do not promise a return or exchange based on this excerpt.

## Prepared AI draft — verify this

“Hi Sam, to connect your L4 USB over Wi-Fi, hold the wireless button until the blue light flashes, then follow the L4 Air setup guide. If wireless printing is essential and this doesn't work for you, contact support to discuss compatible models. Return or exchange eligibility would need to be checked.”

## Your response

1. Which claim needs correcting?
- wireless: The L4 USB can join Wi-Fi using the wireless button and L4 Air guide.
- options: Support can discuss compatible models.
- eligibility: Return or exchange eligibility needs checking.

2. Which source section establishes the connection method for Sam's exact model?
- air: §2 · L4 Air wireless connection
- usb: §1 · L4 USB connection
- policy: §3 · When wireless printing is required

3. Sam says wireless printing is essential. What is the supported next step?
- adapter: Recommend a USB Wi-Fi adapter.
- exchange: Promise a free exchange for an L4 Air.
- discuss: Contact support to discuss compatible models without promising a return or exchange.

4. Write your corrected customer reply in your own words. Explain what Sam's model supports, offer the documented way to print from the laptop, and explain what to do if wireless printing is essential. Do not invent driver steps or policy terms. Written replies are not automatically graded.
