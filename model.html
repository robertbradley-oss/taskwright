THE HABIT

Check who the source is for.

An instruction can be accurately quoted and still be wrong for the customer. Similar model names do not make instructions interchangeable.

1. Identify the exact model

Start with the model the customer actually owns. Check the full name, including any suffix.

2. Match the source

Find the section for that model. A nearby instruction for another version may not apply.

3. Offer supported help

Offer what the source supports. Do not invent workarounds or promises when it cannot meet the request.

CUSTOMER TICKET · FICTIONAL

“Where is the wireless button?”

I have a Vale Label Printer L4 USB. That exact model name is on the underside label. I want to print from my laptop over Wi-Fi. I haven't bought an adapter or tried any setup steps yet. How do I connect it?

— Sam

YOUR SOURCE

Vale L4 series · Manual excerpt

Fictional training material · Revision 1

§1 · L4 USB connection
The L4 USB prints from a computer through a USB cable. It has no Wi-Fi radio or wireless setup button. A USB Wi-Fi adapter does not add wireless printing to this model. For printing from a laptop, connect the included USB cable and follow the L4 USB driver guide for that laptop's operating system.
§2 · L4 Air wireless connection
The L4 Air supports Wi-Fi. Hold its wireless button until the blue light flashes, then follow the L4 Air setup guide. These wireless instructions apply only to the L4 Air.
§3 · When wireless printing is required
If an L4 USB owner needs wireless printing, contact support to discuss compatible models. This excerpt does not establish return eligibility, exchange availability, stock, or pricing. Do not promise a return or exchange based on this excerpt.
PREPARED AI DRAFT · VERIFY THIS
“Hi Sam, to connect your L4 USB over Wi-Fi, hold the wireless button until the blue light flashes, then follow the L4 Air setup guide. If wireless printing is essential and this doesn't work for you, contact support to discuss compatible models. Return or exchange eligibility would need to be checked.”
1. Which claim needs correcting?
2. Which source section establishes the connection method for Sam’s exact model?
3. Sam says wireless printing is essential. What is the supported next step?

Use your own words. Explain what Sam’s model supports, offer the documented way to print from the laptop, and explain what to do if wireless printing is essential. Don’t invent driver steps or policy terms. Your writing is not automatically graded.

ONE SUPPORTED RESPONSE

The right model. An honest next step.

Hi Sam, your L4 USB has no Wi-Fi radio or wireless setup button. The button instructions apply to the L4 Air, and a USB Wi-Fi adapter won’t add wireless printing to your model. To print from your laptop, connect the included USB cable and follow the L4 USB driver guide for your laptop’s operating system. If wireless printing is essential, contact support to discuss compatible models. Return or exchange eligibility would need to be checked.

Evidence: §1 establishes your model’s connection method, §2 identifies the Air-only instructions, and §3 sets the boundary on support and policy advice.
